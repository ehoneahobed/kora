The sync server reports committed writes: `createKoraServer({ onOperationsCommitted(operations, context) })` (also `syncOptions.onOperationsCommitted`) and `server.onOperationsCommitted(handler)` on `KoraSyncServer` and `createProductionServer` (returns an unsubscribe function) run once per batch after it is durably committed. Device uploads over WebSocket and HTTP long-polling arrive with `source: 'sync'` and the session's `auth` and `sessionId`; server-authored writes (`server.kora.apply`, `applyConditional`, `transaction`, `server.access`, the spaces routes, writes a validator makes through `context.kora`, `applyLocalOperation`, `relayServerOperations`) with `source: 'server'`. The referential side effects and cross-record corrections a write causes are in its batch. Refused, ignored and duplicate operations never appear, and a backup restore does not call it. Handlers get a copy of the operations as stored, are not awaited, and fire on the instance that committed; a handler that throws or rejects is logged (`operations.commit_hook_failed`), counted as an error and emitted on the server's `emitter` as the new `server:commit-hook-failed` event, and never affects the commit, the acknowledgment or the fan-out. Apps that wrapped `store.applyRemoteOperation` to see committed writes can use it instead (that wrapper missed transactions, conditional applies and server writes).

An upload batch that stops part way (a store error, the session ending because a grant was deleted) now relays the operations it committed before stopping to the other connected sessions, as a completed batch does; before, a protocol-1 client missed them until it reconnected. A `kora.transaction` and the side effects it causes are fanned out together.

Operations committed before a follow-up step fails (a cascade or set-null write, a cross-record correction, the side effects of a transaction) are now relayed to connected devices and reported to `onOperationsCommitted`; before, a failing follow-up left the committed write unannounced until the next delivery poll (and a protocol-1 client until it reconnected). A `kora.apply` whose follow-up failed still rejects.
