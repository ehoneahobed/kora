Staged replication (first-visit parity S3). `Store.beginStaging()` opens a scratch in-memory replica of the store (`StorageAdapter.createStagingAdapter()`: a second SQLite WASM worker in the browser, `:memory:` on native SQLite) that a large first sync is applied into, and `StagingReplica.merge()` copies it into the live database in time-sliced chunks of whole records (rows, operation log, fold state and provisional effects together, so a crash leaves every record either untouched or complete). A record the live database already knows something about (a write made while replicating) is not copied over: its staged operations replay through the ordinary apply path, which deduplicates by id and folds with the live history. The delivery watermark, quarantine rows and completion mark are written in the last transaction, so the watermark never passes data that is not durable. `StagingReplica.merge()` also supports merging a staged prefix, and replays the replica's buffered apply, merge-trace and constraint events to the live emitter after the commit (bounded; overflow is counted in `eventsDropped`). A replica that cannot be merged faithfully (for example a retracted record that was also written here) is reported as unmergeable and the stream is replicated again from the live watermark.

Review round 2: the clock adopts each chunk's newest stamp (fieldVersions included) before its rows are readable; chunks are also bounded by 4000 operations; `discard()` cancels and awaits an in-flight merge; replica events use separate bounded buffers with summarised overflow (`eventCounts`), a throwing listener is counted (`listenerErrors`) instead of failing the merge, and events of committed chunks are replayed after a failed merge. New `StorageAdapter.prefersStaging` (false on better-sqlite3) and `StagingReplica.sizeBytes()`.

Review round 3: the replica copies the live clock's reference offset and cold/warm state (no false clock drift on a slow device); `discard()` keeps the replica and the overlay guard up until a stuck chunk ends; a retried merge no longer reports records an earlier attempt copied twice.

Review round 4: a merge stopped because the store closed reports `cancelled: true`.
