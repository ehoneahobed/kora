A record deleted on the server while outside a device's view no longer comes back on that device, or on a new device, through a stale write. An operation after which the record is deleted (a delete, or a write older than the record's newest delete) is now delivered by the record's folded state and by what each session's view held of the record, not by the operation's own values: a delete reaches every view in which the record was live at some point (including `scopeExit: 'retain'` devices, retracted records, query-subset views and access-rules members who joined after the last write), and a stale write is sent only to such a view, when its values are in view, after the record's newest delete (sent once per delivery pass).

The decision costs one batched read of a bounded per-record summary (`kora_record_live_views`, kept in each write's transaction over the fields sessions register at their handshake), never the record's history. Scope snapshots gain an additive `deleted` field. Both are filled for existing data by a background backfill after start (short, resumable transactions; no startup rebuild, the snapshot fingerprint is unchanged, so a rolling deploy or a rollback never wipes snapshots). Until a record's summary and tombstone state are known (and for records last written by an older instance) delivery is as in 1.0.0-beta.14. Stores gain the optional `registerLiveViewFields`, `getRecordLiveViews`, `requestLiveViewRebuild` and `whenLiveViewsIdle`; a custom store without them keeps the previous behavior.
