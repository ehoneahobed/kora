A record deleted on the server while outside a device's view no longer comes back on that device, or on a new device, through a stale write. An operation after which the record is deleted (a delete, or a write older than the record's newest delete) is now delivered by the record's folded state and by what each session's view held of the record, not by the operation's own values: a delete reaches every view in which the record was live at some point (including `scopeExit: 'retain'` devices, retracted records, query-subset views and access-rules members who joined after the last write), and a stale write is sent only to such a view, when its values are in view, after the record's newest delete (sent once per delivery pass).

The decision costs one batched read of a bounded per-record summary (`kora_record_live_views`) over the fields the server's own grants read, never the record's history. A summary is trusted only while the record's fold state records that it accounts for the record's newest operation (`lv_through` beside `covered_op_id`); a write by an instance that does not maintain it (an older version during a rolling deploy), a re-fold or a restore leaves it unknown until a background rebuild, and a later write never makes it current again. A write that changes neither the record's liveness nor a tracked value touches no summary. Scope snapshots gain an additive `deleted` field. Summaries and the tombstone state of existing data are filled by a background backfill after start (short, guarded transactions; no startup rebuild, the snapshot fingerprint is unchanged). While unknown, a delete is delivered as in 1.0.0-beta.14, and a write older than the record's delete is delivered as before when its values are in view, preceded by the record's newest delete (the fold keeps its fields, so a device without it would diverge after a revival); for an access-rules collection, only to a member whose membership began before the record was deleted (judged at the first delete after its last live write, so a second delete does not move it; withheld when that delete cannot be pinned down). Tracked fields come from the server's grant before any client narrowing; a replace-mode restore or a snapshot wipe bumps an epoch that voids rebuilds in flight; a fold state invalidated in place (`covered_seq = -1`) leaves its summary unknown. Stores gain the optional `registerLiveViewFields`, `getRecordLiveViews`, `requestLiveViewRebuild` and `whenLiveViewsIdle`; a custom store without them keeps the previous behavior. A failed read of where a record's deletion began withholds the write and is logged (`session.tombstone_lookup_failed`), counted in `errorCount` and emitted as the new `server:tombstone-lookup-failed` event.
